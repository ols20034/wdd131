Overview

Purpose

create a website for my personal club that shows information about the club, its members, and upcoming events. Provide information about what it was like to live during the 1800's as a mountainman in the west. The website will also include information for past events with photos, descriptions of how to participate in future events. I will include a contact page for people to ask questions and get in touch with the club along with the current Executive Committee members.

Audience

General public interested in the history of the mountainmen of the American frontier.

Content

Home Page

Fort Buenaventura Mountainman is a historical reenactment club dedicated to preserving the skills, stories, and lifestyle of the 1800s American frontier. The home page introduces visitors to who we are, what we do, and why the Mountainman era continues to inspire people today. It highlights upcoming events, showcases photos from past gatherings, and provides quick links to learn more about the club.

Images for the Home page

Events

The Events page lists all upcoming and past Mountainman gatherings, including rendezvous, skill‑building workshops, black powder shoots, and historical demonstrations. Each event includes dates, descriptions, and photos. Visitors can learn how to participate, what to bring, and how to get involved with future reenactments.

Images for the Events page

Download Rendezvous Flyer

Contact

The Contact page provides a simple form for visitors to reach out with questions about the club, membership, or upcoming events. It also lists the current Executive Committee members with their names, roles, and photos so visitors know who to contact for specific needs.

Wireframes

Below are the wireframes for each page of the Fort Buenaventura Mountainman website.

Home

The Home page includes a hero image of the Mountainman camp, an introduction to the club, and two featured images (camp scene + Fort Buenaventura map). It also includes quick links to the Events and Contact pages.

Events

The Events page displays upcoming and past events in a simple list layout. Each event includes a title, date, description, and an accompanying image (such as Rendezvous photos or black powder demonstrations).

Contact

The Contact page includes a contact form, club email, park address, and a section listing the Executive Committee members with their names, roles, and phone numbers.
